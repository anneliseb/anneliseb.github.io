Beyond the pixels

Good design practice requires knowing your zoom level. Some problems demand a view of the full system — the service, the handoffs, the parts nobody owns. Others ask you to slow down and work through a single moment until it's exactly right. The skill isn't in the zooming — it's in knowing when to do which.

My design superpower is alignment. There's something powerful about creating a visual artifact that lets a room full of people — engineers, product managers, executives — finally see the same thing and move forward together. That's where design earns its leverage.

I'm energized by discovery and the collaborative work that makes it possible — facilitating workshops, building shared understanding, and helping teams figure out what they're really solving for before committing to a solution. I'm also increasingly interested in AI as a design material: what does it mean to design an experience where AI is the product, not just the assistant?
