An introduction

I am a problem solver at heart. Rather than stick to a set process, I adapt to the current situation and pull from my ever growing toolkit. No designer can do it alone and nothing makes me happier at work than getting to work with a group of smart, fun individuals. Facilitating interactive collaboration sessions is one of my specialities.

When I’m not designing, I can be found at the ice rink, figure skating, in the gym, or at home cuddling with my pup, Finnegan, while getting caught up in a good book . I love to travel and some of my favorite memories are rafting down the sava bohinjka in Slovenia, enjoying great wine in Istria, exploring art galleries in Santa Fe, and hiking a glacier in Iceland.
