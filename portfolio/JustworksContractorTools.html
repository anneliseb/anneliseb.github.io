Contractor Tools Discovery

Developing a strategy on what it would take for Justworks to earn the right to charge customers for contractors.

My role

  • Led research to understand what small businesses value when managing and paying contractors/vendors domestically and internationally
  • Partnered with product, engineering, and product marketing to create business case and recommendation, presenting to senior leadership
  • Created vision to help guide conversations across leadership and to align on a possible future

Results

  • Vision prototype was received by SVP of Product who utilized in in company strategy conversations
  • Received by-in from leadership to create a Bill Pay pod, focused on creating a new accounts payable experience from 0–1
  • Partnered closely throughout discovery initiative with international which operated as a separate entity, closing the gap between them and the rest of technology

Context

Background

39% of customers paid contractors through Justworks, and data trends showed an increase in contractors within the workforce in coming years. Most competitors charge customers for paying contractors, but many do not provide specific tooling. Justworks identified charging for contractors as a potential revenue growth area.

Problem

JJustworks leadership had many open questions regarding contractors such as should we build tools for contractors and/or build tools for companies and should we build, buy, or partner? I along with my GPM and Sr EM partners were asked to work with product marketing and develop consumer insights to understand different use cases across contractor and company types and to understand what our customers and / or their contractors find most useful.
