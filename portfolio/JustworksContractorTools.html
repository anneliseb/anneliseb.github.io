Contractor Tools Discovery

Developing a strategy on what it would take for Justworks to earn the right to charge customers for contractors.

My role

  • Led research to understand what small businesses value when managing and paying contractors/vendors domestically and internationally
  • Partnered with product, engineering, and product marketing to create business case and recommendation, presenting to senior leadership
  • Created vision to help guide conversations across leadership and to align on a possible future

Results

  • Vision prototype was received by SVP of Product who utilized in in company strategy conversations
  • Received by-in from leadership to create a Bill Pay pod, focused on creating a new accounts payable experience from 0–1
  • Partnered closely throughout discovery initiative with international which operated as a separate entity, closing the gap between them and the rest of technology

Context

Background

39% of customers paid contractors through Justworks, and data trends showed an increase in contractors within the workforce in coming years. Most competitors charge customers for paying contractors, but many do not provide specific tooling. Justworks identified charging for contractors as a potential revenue growth area.

Problem

Justworks leadership had many open questions regarding contractors such as should we build tools for contractors and/or build tools for companies and should we build, buy, or partner? I along with my GPM and Sr EM partners were asked to work with product marketing and develop consumer insights to understand different use cases across contractor and company types and to understand what our customers and / or their contractors find most useful.

Customer Research

I led all aspects of our customer research include developing our research questions, deciding on the best methodologies to answer our research questions, creating the research brief, writing the discussion guide, moderating user interviews, and analyzing quantitative data. Below are some examples of research findings included in the final report.

Target customer

  • 1. Small size: less than 50 employees
  • 2. Flexible work: companies hat have project or client based work
  • 3. Founder as admin: A founder or cofounder is responsible for HR and Ops tasks
  • 4. Cost-conscious: scrappy and looking for places to save money

Not target customer

  • 1. System of record: already has a system of record outside of Justworks
  • 2. Bill.com user: established process of using Bill.com for all invoicing
  • 3. Accounting firm process: Accounting firm dictates tolling and processes
  • 4. Financial leadership: May be willing to switch but will require robust product offering

Invoicing is a key workflow

On average there were ~6 tools used to complete a single workflow. Because the tools don’t always integrate with each other, much of the process can become manual to get information from one tool inputted to another, introducing the risk of human error.

At the very minimum, the expectation is an invoice can be attached to a contractor/vendor payment. There is an opportunity to dive further into the bill pay space and bring the customer’s processes back into Justworks.

Justworks keeps companies compliant

Many customers have come to trust Justworks and rely on Justworks to stay compliant and that’s important when working with contractors as well. In a Pendo survey the second biggest pain point selected was remaining compliant (21.7%).

We also saw a trend of admins unable to remember what tax form is required by contractors and if JW creates them or not.

Parity between domestic and international

From the customer’s POV, there is no distinction in how they manage an intl vs domestic contractor. The expectation is the experience in Justworks will be the same for managing contractors regardless of country.

One customer noted International contractor experience in Justworks feels rather slim in comparison to domestic.

Vision Development

The research report was shared and presented at a cross-functional workshop between the team working on this discovery effort and international. The goals of the workshop was to use the findings to co-create on a future contractor experience taking into account both domestic and international workers. I used the workshop sketches to further develop a narrative and to create a vision protoype.

Content workshop

Final presentation & Outcomes

The GPM, Sr Eng Manager, and I partnered on a presentation to give to senior leadership across Technology, Strategy, and International. Our recommendation was to create a tiered offering for contractor management that scales in price and value with our customer, and combines domestic and international capabilities into a seamless experience. The presentation was well received and Technology leadership approved the creation of a new pod, focusing on Bill Pay, but only for domestic contractors and not international.

Reflection

I learned invaluable lessons on how to prepare for and present to senior leadership at a company.

While the vision prototype was very well received, I created it after sensing a lack of alignment across leadership. Having a more robust prototype during the leadership presentation may have been helpful.

While we made strides towards connecting with the international team, at the end of the dayy the decision was made for us to not work together and not disrupt international from their goals.
